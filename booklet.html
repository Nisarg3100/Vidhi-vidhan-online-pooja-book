Step-by-Step Booking Process

  1. Visit Our Website

    Open your preferred browser and go to www.vidhividhan.com or download our mobile app from the Play Store or App Store.

  2. Click the Instant Booking Button

    On our homepage, click the prominent "Instant Booking" button to begin your seamless booking experience.

  3. Select Your Pooja & Pandit

    Browse our extensive catalog of 100+ poojas and rituals. Filter by occasion, deity, or duration. View pandit profiles with ratings and experience before selecting.

  4. Enter Your Details

    Provide your contact information, preferred date/time, and location details. Add any special instructions for the pandit regarding the ceremony.

  5. Secure Payment

    Choose from multiple secure payment options including credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, or wallets. Your payment is protected with 256-bit SSL encryption.

  6. Receive Confirmation

    Get instant confirmation via SMS and email with all booking details. Our team will also call to verify your booking within 30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a pooja?

We recommend booking at least 3-5 days in advance to ensure pandit availability, especially for popular dates and festivals. However, we also accommodate last-minute bookings based on availability.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule my booking?

You can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the scheduled pooja time through your account dashboard or by calling our support team. Cancellation fees may apply depending on timing.

Do I need to provide anything for the pooja?

Our pandits bring all essential pooja materials. However, you may want to prepare fruits, flowers, or specific offerings as per your tradition. The pandit will guide you when they confirm the booking.
